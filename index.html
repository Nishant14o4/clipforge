4. Audio

Game sound and player voice cannot be separated if they are mixed in one video. If you have the player voice as a separate file, add it here and you can control both volumes. The voice file must be in sync with the video.

6. Swear-word mute

Manual mute (exact time)

0 places will be muted.

The browser cannot recognize words in audio, so automatic muting works from the timing of your subtitle lines (game or player track). Import an .srt first or add lines (an .srt made by CapCut or Whisper works). The time of a word inside a line is only estimated, so increase the padding or use Manual mute for exact timing.

7. Hook text, watermark and logo

Best moments

First choose a video and click Find best moments.

Manual cut

Use the seek bar or play the video and stop where you want, set Start and End, then click Add clip. You do not need to scan for best moments first.

Each clip exports in the background, so you can keep previewing while it runs. Tick Join on two or more clips and use Export joined clips to make one video (this is also how you cut out a middle part: make two clips and join them).
